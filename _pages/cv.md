### Bio

If you need a four-sentence bio about me:


> Felix Koehler is a research scientist at VIRDX and QuantCo, where he is working on biophysical simulations and deep learning systems for non-invasive tissue characterization using medical imaging. He holds a PhD in machine learning for physics simulations from the Technical University of Munich under the supervision of Prof. Nils Thuerey. His research interests span numerical simulations of PDEs, reduced-order models, neural emulators, optimization theory, automatic differentiation, adjoint methods, and inverse problems. Besides research, he creates free educational material covering Machine Learning & Simulation on his YouTube channel.
